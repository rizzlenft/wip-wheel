## Form draft (edit in your voice)

**Project name:** The WIP Wheel

**Who are you?**  
Rizzle. I host The WIP Meetup, a weekly web3 metaverse meetup since 2019 (Thursdays, 12 PM PT). I already bring that community through Decentraland. I also ship browser games at rizzle.io/games.

**What is the game?**  
A live game-show studio inside our weekly meetup. Gold pad: one daily chance spin, small prize on the left wall. Pink pad: Wheel of Fortune for the Thursday 12 PM PT show — jackpot, NFT, letter board. The right wall is the show board: countdown to the live episode, called letters, contestant scores, and the bank. Starter show format is one guest, one spin, then call a letter. Later we expand to three contestants and a public wheel address so guest speakers can fund a slice.

**Why will people come back tomorrow?**  
There is a daily spin with smaller prizes, so the World is not dead six days a week. The in-world countdown and guest/prize hype make Thursday feel like an episode you do not want to miss. The WIP Meetup is already that Thursday habit. The wheel is the ritual in the middle of it.

**What are you testing in V0?**  
Whether people will drop in for the daily spin, whether the countdown actually pulls them to the live show, and whether WIP Meetup attendees treat the wheel as part of the Thursday hang instead of a side curiosity.
