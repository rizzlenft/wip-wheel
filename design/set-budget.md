## Order from here

### 1. One studio kit

Researched against Wheel of Fortune’s set (cyc, LED board, lit ring around the wheel, contestants by the wheel, audience behind) and the Belgian studio version (cyc wash, LED edges on the stairs and banquettes, prize plinths, cameras). Most of that is big planes and glowing edges.

Build those from scene primitives in the navy / gold / magenta paints. They cost no texture. A new Creator Hub model is allowed only when boxes cannot make the shape, and only one new image. The same file may be copied. A second file that embeds the same picture still counts as another texture, so the sci-fi pack is not free just because the models share `SciFiPack_TX.png`.

The sci-fi surveillance cameras were placed and removed. The spots were fine; the model was not. Neon tubes, floor tiles, the LED-grid wall, hanging pendants, video screens, and string lights do not make the cut. The puzzle board is already the LED wall. The speakers stay. They cost nothing.

The string-light file cannot become that rig. It is one short loose strand. A grid is truss plus lamp heads on the atlas. The four real lights stay StageLight, FillLight, AccentLight, and the cabinet spot.

### 2. What the kit replaces, in order

1. Cyc wings, a gold truss around the banner, a floor ring, and three chairs are in. Matte albedo, no new light. Pad 1’s spin volume is unchanged.
2. A studio bench in place of the sci-fi rows, with the same four sit spots. The camera model was removed.
3. Cabinet swap is done. The shell is in. Do not spend a texture slot on another one-off prop beside the altar.

Wheel, banner, letter tiles, logo, and the daily face stay the images they already are. Letter sizes do not change.

### 3. Rules

- Model it low from the start. The pedestal is the daily host; do not bring the cabinet mesh back.
- Big surfaces take color from albedo. Roughness near 1, metallic 0, emissive off. That is the rough-stone recipe, and it is why the wheel glare came off. Emissive is only for a small accent, like the pedestal rune. A glow on a whole prop blooms white and does not light anything around it.
- The four lights stay StageLight, FillLight, AccentLight, and the cabinet spot. A dark face gets a lighter albedo, not a fifth light and not an emissive fill.
- One shared atlas. A new prop does not get its own texture.
- No skinned meshes, no video screen, no blended transparency.
- One file for a repeated prop. Do not export `chair_01.glb`, `chair_02.glb`.
- Stop near 24,000 triangles. The hard cap is 40,000.

### 4. Before a show

Publish at least an hour ahead so asset-bundle conversion finishes. In the world, `/showfps` with people standing in the room. Under 30 means the last model waits.
